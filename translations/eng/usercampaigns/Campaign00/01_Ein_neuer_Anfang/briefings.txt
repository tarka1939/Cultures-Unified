Life in our village was calm and peaceful, when suddenly King Bjarni said:
My men have grown lazy and fat. It is time we put to sea again and had new adventures. Come on, ready a ship and set sail. In the end he found only one loyal follower willing to sail with Bjarni. So the two of them put to sea alone.......

At last we found a small island. Perhaps there were women here willing to marry ?.....

King Bjarni ordered me:
1. Build a new village
2. Store 20 longswords, 20 plate armours, 20 chain mails and 20 shortswords in your warehouses.
Also, 20 big healing potions must be in the warehouse.
What did Bjarni want with so many weapons ?
Did he know more than he cared to admit ?
Well, first we had to find willing girls to marry, then we would try to fulfil Bjarni's wish.
