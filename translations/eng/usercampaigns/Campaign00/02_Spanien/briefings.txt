At last we had arrived in Spain. Our helmsman was so drunk that he had run onto a reef off the coast. We could only save a few chests of weapons, which Bjarni did not like one bit.
Furious, he gave us the following order:
Now store 40 longswords, 40 amulets of defence and 40 plate armours in the warehouses.
Furthermore, he ordered, the Portuguese live in the west.
Destroy them all !!
But why we had to sail to Spain, he did not say.
Well, grumbling, we got to work. But where were we to get the amulets?
We decided to send out a scout. Perhaps there were still peoples here we could trade with..........
